# Privacy

Javelin Mail does not collect account information, messages, contacts, calendars, usage data,
diagnostics, or telemetry. The Javelin Mail project does not operate a service that receives this
data.

Your account data and settings are stored on your computer. Javelin connects directly to the mail
servers you configure in order to provide its mail, contacts, and calendar features. It does not
send your data elsewhere for advertising, analytics, profiling, or crash reporting.

Google Translate is an optional exception. If you choose Google Translate as your translation
provider and request a translation, the text to be translated is sent directly to Google. Automatic
translation rules that you explicitly enable may also send matching message text to Google. This
feature is disabled unless you opt in, and Google's handling of that text is governed by Google's
own terms and privacy policy.
